cDocs — Document Viewing see cDocs module on board Three callers that sign user-opened document URLs now route through a new method that enforces a 10-minute minimum for China-bucket files: the document download-URL endpoint, Document#file_url, and the document bundle view. For global-bucket tenants the behaviour is identical to before. For China-bucket tenants the signed expiry grows — the fix only improves availability, but any existing TC that asserts the exact expiry value or exercises the download path on a China tenant is now validating changed behaviour.
Common App — Submissions see Common App Submissions module on board The specific reported failure (CC-32586) was a counselor on a China tenant clicking an Included File(s) link on the One & Done submission page. That link goes through the document download-URL endpoint, which is one of the three changed callers. Any TC that covers opening or previewing a document from the Submissions tab should be re-run on a China tenant to confirm the fix and confirm no regression on global tenants.

TP-001 cDocs — Document ViewingDocuments tab — China tenant Access to a China-region tenant (a school whose uploaded files are stored in the China S3 bucket). A counselor account on that tenant. At least one student with an uploaded PDF document.
  1. Log in as a counselor on the China tenant.
  2. Open a student's profile and navigate to their Documents tab.
  3. Click on a PDF document to view or open it.
  4. Wait at least 35 seconds without interacting with the page (this is longer than the previous 30-second link lifetime).
  5. Scroll through or interact with the PDF to confirm it has fully loaded.
 The PDF opens and loads completely without a 'Failed to load PDF document' or network error. The document remains accessible and readable after the original 30-second window has passed. China-region tenant counselor credentials. At least one student with an uploaded PDF on that tenant. Ideally tested from within China or via a proxy that routes traffic through the China CDN path.
